Features

Built for fluid reading, language learning, and sentence mining on mobile

Works Over Any App

Horizontal and vertical text, portrait and landscape. The popup positions itself dynamically where space allows and never covers the word you are looking up.

62 Languages & Yomitan Dictionaries

Full dictionary support across 62 languages. Structured definitions, examples, images, inflections, pitch accent graphs, Chinese Pinyin and Cantonese Jyutping with tone marks, frequency lists, and character breakdowns.

Screen & Camera OCR

Recognize text on screen with cloud recognition, instant on-device drafts, or offline on-device models (ML Kit, PaddleOCR, Tesseract). Snap a photo with the built-in camera or open saved images to pan, zoom, and look up text.

1-Tap Anki Cards

Seamless export through AnkiDroid: customizable note types, field templates, duplicate detection and updates, pronunciation audio, screen captures, or photo crops.

Audio & Pronunciation

Native pronunciation audio from Yomitan community sources, custom local audio endpoints, or system text-to-speech (TTS) fallback.

Private, Offline & Customizable

Forced Offline Mode for complete privacy. Customizable color themes (Material You, presets, custom palette), font adjustments, e-ink high contrast mode, and zero ads, trackers, or accounts.

Independent Language Profiles

Switch between favorite languages instantly from Home or the floating bubble's hold menu. Each language maintains its own independent settings:

  • Separate Dictionaries: Configure active dictionaries, priority order, and definition languages per profile.
  • Tailored OCR Engines: Choose optimal recognition models (ML Kit, PaddleOCR, Tesseract) and script directions for each language.
  • Dedicated Anki Templates: Map cards to language-specific Anki note types, decks, and field templates.
  • Interface in 17 Languages: Complete UI translation into English, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, German, French, and 10 more languages.

How to Install

Get started with Screenlate on your Android phone or tablet

  1. Download the APK: Get the latest version from GitHub Releases. arm64-v8a fits modern smartphones; armeabi-v7a, x86_64, and universal are also available (Android 7.0+ supported; can also be tracked via Obtainium).
  2. Enable Accessibility Service: Open Screenlate and grant the accessibility service permission to allow the floating bubble and screen scanning.
  3. Choose your languages & dictionaries: Pick your target languages and download dictionaries in one tap from the built-in catalog, or import your existing Yomitan .zip collections.
