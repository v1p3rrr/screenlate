# Group 4b: Hindi, Bengali, Georgian, Armenian (plan of 2026-10-19)

Part of the languages work (`2026-10-10-languages-implementation.md`, Stage E, question 30). That plan's decision
rows bind here; the ones this plan builds on are listed below by name, and changes to them are recorded there.
Research: `notes/languages.md`, "Group 4b research".

## Goal

Four languages with scripts of their own, written left to right with spaces between words, each looked up, read by
OCR, voiced and exported as the group 4a languages are:

- Hindi (Devanagari): ML Kit's Devanagari model bundled, Tesseract `hin` offered (Paddle devanagari once uploaded).
- Bengali (Bengali script): Tesseract `ben` (ML Kit's Devanagari package holds a Bengali model, to be measured).
- Georgian: Tesseract `kat`, Yomitan's Georgian rules.
- Armenian: Tesseract `hye`.

## Questions

1. Answered: (a), the agent's choice at the owner's request ("I don't know it, do what is best"). Hindi spellings. Wiktionary writes nukta (पढ़ना, बड़ा) and candrabindu (हूँ, माँ); much printed and typed text
   writes no nukta (पढना) and anusvara instead (हूं, मां), and none of those spellings is in the dictionary.
   (a, recommended) A word without nukta also matches headwords with it, a word with nukta matches exactly (as the
   Greek tonos); anusvara and candrabindu match each other both ways. Pairs that then show together: खाना (food) and
   ख़ाना (compartment), हंस (swan) and हँस (laugh). (b) Only anusvara and candrabindu; nukta exact. (c) Exact spellings
   only.
2. Answered: (a). Bengali endings. Wiktionary lists few Bengali forms (53.6k rows), so বইটির ("of the book") finds nothing. (a,
   recommended) Our own list of about 30 noun endings (-টি -টা -গুলো -র -কে -তে -ে …), so the word without its ending
   is looked up too: of the 20,000 commonest words 9,058 instead of 6,697 find an entry; a word that is itself a
   headword still comes first. (b) Only the forms the dictionary lists.
3. Answered: (a). Scan length. The popup looks at most 16 letters from the tap; 5% of Georgian and Armenian word forms are longer
   (ժողովրդավարություն, 18), so they are not found whole. (a, recommended) 24 by default for Georgian and Armenian, 16
   for the others (the setting stays per language). (b) 16 for all.
4. Answered: (a), the agent's choice at the owner's request. Branch order. (a, recommended) Hindi, Bengali, Georgian, Armenian: the Devanagari and Bengali pieces first, the
   two Tesseract-only languages after. (b) Another order.
5. Answered: (b), the popular ones, after the question was asked again in plainer words. Fonts. No downloadable font covers these scripts; the system has Noto Sans and Noto Serif for each. (a,
   recommended) The system fonts only: sans by default, the serif one through the font list. (b) Also downloadable
   fonts for Hindi and Bengali (Hind and Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Hind Siliguri and Tiro Bangla; OFL, 0.2-0.5 MB each);
   Georgian and Armenian have only Noto anyway.
6. Answered: (a). The English name of bn. Android says "Bangla" (and Vietnamese "Tiếng Bangla"); Wiktionary, the translators'
   menus and the other interface languages say Bengali (бенгальский). (a, recommended) "Bengali" in English and
   Vietnamese; (b) "Bangla" as Android.
7. Answered: (a). The Bengali flag (one flag per language): (a, recommended) Bangladesh; (b) India.
8. Answered: (a), the agent's choice at the owner's request. Bubble letters: (a, recommended) the first letter of the language's own name: हि (Hindi), বা (Bangla), ქ (Kartuli),
   Հ (Hayeren); (b) the first letter of the alphabet: अ, অ, ა, Ա (अ and অ look alike).
9. Answered: later, together with the next Paddle models. The Paddle Devanagari model (7.9 MB with its character list), for the `ocr-models-1` release. ML Kit reads Hindi
   best (1.8% errors against Paddle's 2.7%), so Paddle would be listed unticked. (a, recommended) Upload it: the
   files are `paddle-devanagari.zip` with `model.mnn`, `dict.txt`, `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`. (b) No upload; Hindi lists
   ML Kit and Tesseract.
10. Asked after the measurement on the Bengali branch: Bengali's recommended model if ML Kit does not read Bengali.
    The rule picks Tesseract fast (34.7% errors over clean and hard screens against 27.2% for best, under a third
    fewer), while on clean screens best makes 0.5% against 6.3% (11.1 MB against 0.9 MB).

  session first).
