# Group 4c: Thai, Khmer, Lao (plan of 2026-10-19)

Part of the languages work (`2026-10-10-languages-implementation.md`, Stage E, question 30). That plan's decision
rows bind here; the ones this plan builds on are listed below by name, and changes to them are recorded there.
Research: `notes/languages.md`, "Group 4c research".

## Goal

Languages written without spaces between words, each looked up from the word under the tap, read by OCR, voiced and
exported as the other languages are:

- Thai: Paddle's Thai recognizer once the owner releases the waiting models, Tesseract `tha` until then; Lingua Libre
  recordings and Google's voice; a frequency dictionary from PyThaiNLP.
- Khmer: Tesseract `khm` on the phone and cloud recognition; Google's voice.
- Lao: deferred, for a while or for good (row "Lao and Khmer"). Had it been built: Tesseract `lao` on the phone
  (weak) and cloud recognition; no recordings or voice found.

## Decisions this plan builds on

Rows of the languages plan: "Scope of the big release", "Depth", "On-device recognition" (cloud only is not wanted),
"Catalog threshold", "Transcription threshold", "Own-language dictionaries", "Recommended dictionaries", "Frequency
dictionaries" (built on the phone), "On-device OCR engines" (models measured as unusable are not offered), "OCR
engine choice" (the recommendation rule), "Tesseract models", "Paddle model hosting" (upload after the owner approves
the file list), "OCR model list", "Flags", "Flag country" (a default flag only: Thailand, Cambodia, Laos), "Bubble
letters", "Audio order, groups 2 and 3" (by measured coverage), "Text to speech" (last source), "Font lists across
languages", "Vietnamese words of several syllables" (the model for earlier starts), "Vietnamese classifiers" (the
model for Thai and Lao classifier rows). Row "Scan length" of `2026-10-19-group-4b.md`: 16 by default for these
languages (1.9% of Thai headwords are longer, 0.9% of Khmer, 0.1% of Lao).

Technical choices of the agent (the owner may object; recorded in the changelog when changed). Lao is deferred (row
"Lao and Khmer"); what these rows and the decisions say about Lao applies when it is taken up:

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Word starts | The lookup starts at the start of the word under the aim as Android's ICU word segmentation finds it, and at the starts of the two ICU words before it; a word from an earlier start that reaches the aimed one comes first, the earliest start first, then the aimed word's own results (the Vietnamese rule). Two earlier words reach every part of 96% of Thai headwords; three lookups per tap (owner, 2026-10-19, question 1, after asking whether it is the most optimal and intuitive of the four: yes) |
| Anki sentence | Thai and Lao: a space between two letters of the script ends the sentence, except next to the repetition mark (also after it), any ฯ (not only ฯลฯ), digits and Latin words; ? ! … and line ends as well. Khmer: ។ ៕ ? ! … (owner, 2026-10-19, question 2; ฯ and ๆ kept as the variants with the fewest wrong cuts and merges on ORCHID and the Thai ALT, owner, 2026-10-20, questions 10 and 11: "проверь как адекватнее будет, в каком варианте меньше неправильных определений границ предложений") |
| Thai frequency | Built on the phone from PyThaiNLP's `tnc_freq.txt` (Thai National Corpus counts, CC0) at a pinned commit, checked by SHA-256, as the wordfreq lists are; Lao and Khmer have none (owner, 2026-10-19, question 3) |
| Paddle models upload | The Thai model and every other converted model that waits (Devanagari included) go up in one release later, when the owner decides; until then the catalog lists none of them and Thai offers Tesseract (owner, 2026-10-19, question 4) |
| Lao and Khmer | Thai and Khmer now, branches in the order Thai, Khmer; Khmer offers Tesseract on the phone. Lao is deferred, for a while or for good: one English dictionary of 2,300 words, Tesseract errors above those of the Paddle Latin model rejected for Vietnamese, no recordings or voice. The app is to say somewhere that on-device recognition of these languages may be poor; the wording and the place are settled later (question 9) (owner, 2026-10-19, question 5, first all three now, changed the same day after a summary of the three; replaces "later Lao, Khmer" of the languages plan's question 30) |
| Fonts | The system Noto Sans and Serif by default; downloadable: Thai Sarabun and Noto Sans Thai Looped (looped), Kanit and Prompt (loopless); Khmer Kantumruy Pro and Hanuman; Lao Noto Sans Lao Looped (OFL, from their authors' repositories as the other catalog fonts) (owner, 2026-10-19, question 6) |
| Shorter matches | The longest match from a start always shows; shorter ones from the same start only when they end at an ICU word boundary (การตลาด and การ stay, กา and ก go). Tested so that short words are not lost (owner, 2026-10-19, question 7) |
| Bubble letters | ท (Thai), ລ (Lao), ខ (Khmer): a letter of each language's own name, three distinct shapes (agent's choice at the owner's request, "whatever is better for recognizing the language", 2026-10-19, question 8) |
| Starts inside a word | When nothing found from the aimed ICU word's start or the earlier starts covers the aimed letter, the lookup also starts at each cluster inside the word up to it; the words of the first start that cover the letter come first (ពេញ in ក្រុងភ្នំពេញ shows ភ្នំពេញ, then ក្រុង). Measured as the best of the three variants for the first word shown (owner, 2026-10-20, question 12: "проверь как адекватнее будет") |
| Audio order | Lingua Libre, Wiktionary, then the voice for Thai and Khmer; a source would be left out of a language's defaults only where Commons has no recording of the language at all, which is the case for no language (Khmer: 13 and 9) (owner, 2026-10-20, question 14: "Если 100% вообще нет, то скипаем для таких") |
| Emulator space | Sessions may delete, on the emulator, the dictionaries of languages already checked; AnkiDroid and other data stay untouched (owner, 2026-10-20, question 13) |
| Busy-screen line | Under each Tesseract model of every language (owner, 2026-10-28, question 16; until then Thai and Khmer only), in Bubble → On-device model and on the download screen: "Often misreads text on busy screens; cloud recognition is more accurate." (ru "Часто ошибается на сложных экранах; облачное распознавание точнее."); Paddle's Thai model has none (owner, 2026-10-21, question 9) |

## Measurements (2026-10-22)

### Engines on the emulator

Every engine offered for the two languages, on the OCR comparison screens (`testdata/ocr/compare`; per language two
clean page screens and four hard ones: a decorative font, italic, a photo backdrop, a tilted block), with the harness
of `languages/ocr-compare` (`experiments/ocr-compare`, `OcrCompareTest`) on the Pixel 10 Pro emulator. Errors:
`scripts/ocr-compare/score.py`, characters with spaces ignored. Taps right: a tap at the centre of each ground-truth
word lands on the nearest recognized character of the line there (Tesseract's clusters joined by `ocrSpaceGap`;
character positions from the engine's boxes or the word box split as `TextLayout` splits it) and is right when the
line's text has the word at that character; in brackets, with the bundle's readings (ํา as ำ, subscript TA as DA).
Time: the median of three runs per screen (cloud: one), averaged over the screens. ML Kit reads neither script.
Paddle's Thai model ran on ONNX Runtime, as the harness APK has no MNN library; the app runs it on MNN (2026-10-16:
the same errors, 1.1 / 0.9 s).

- The errors match the run of 2026-10-16/17 within 0.2 points, so the recommendations stand: Tesseract fast for both
  (best is no better and 5-7 times larger), Paddle for Thai once released.
- The clean Khmer times are the emulator's: the first runs took 2.3-6.8 s, the fastest 0.5 s (fast) and 1.1 s
  (best), as on 2026-10-16 (0.6 and 1.2 s).
- Tesseract's misses on the clean Thai screens: dropped tone marks (ที่ as ที, นี้ as นี, ซื้อ as ซือ), ช้า read as
  ข้า, ซ้า or ชํา, and a few words read right whose characters the even split of a word box misplaces (ดี, มาก); the
  ํา reading brings back four words (กําหนด). On the Khmer ones it drops vowel signs (យើង as យេង, ណាស់ as ណាស), and
  the split misplaces more, as its words are whole phrases; the TA/DA reading brings back two or three.
- The busy-screen line (question 9) holds: on the hard screens Tesseract misreads a third of the Thai characters and
  more than half of the Khmer ones and finds half and a quarter of the tapped words; cloud reads them all, Paddle's
  Thai model 98%.
