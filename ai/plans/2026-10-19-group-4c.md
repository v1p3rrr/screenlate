# Group 4c: Thai, Khmer, Lao (plan of 2026-10-19)

Part of the languages work (`2026-10-10-languages-implementation.md`, Stage E, question 30). That plan's decision
rows bind here; the ones this plan builds on are listed below by name, and changes to them are recorded there.
Research: `notes/languages.md`, "Group 4c research".

## Goal

Languages written without spaces between words, each looked up from the word under the tap, read by OCR, voiced and
exported as the other languages are:

- Thai: Paddle's Thai recognizer once the owner releases the waiting models, Tesseract `tha` until then; Lingua Libre
  recordings and Google's voice; a frequency dictionary from PyThaiNLP.
- Khmer: Tesseract `khm` on the phone and cloud recognition; Google's voice.
- Lao: deferred, for a while or for good (row "Lao and Khmer"). Had it been built: Tesseract `lao` on the phone
  (weak) and cloud recognition; no recordings or voice found.

| Topic | Choice |
|---|---|
| Branches | One per language, chained from `languages/main` in the order of the row "Lao and Khmer"; `languages/main` fast-forwarded after each, the ai/ changes copied to `main`. |

| Lao text | ໍາ read as ຳ (text processor); ຫນ ຫມ ຫລ and ໜ ໝ ຫຼ match each other (a text processor one way, headwords keyed the other way at import, as Hindi's `HeadwordMarks.replacements`); Thai letters of the same shape in a Lao word (า ด from cloud recognition) written as the Lao ones (`ocrWord`, as the Cyrillic look-alikes). |
| Khmer text | Subscript DA ្ដ and TA ្ត match each other (text processor and import keys, as Lao's spellings); zero-width spaces dropped from the looked-up text and taken as word boundaries. |
| Form-of rows | Thai and Lao `classifier` rows left out as the Vietnamese ones (1,900 Thai rows: คน would bring 386 entries); Morse code links dropped; duplicate rows merged. |
| Bundles | th, lo, km built from Yomitan's descriptors, which have no text processors or transforms; Thai gets the app's own processors (row "Thai text"). |
| Sentence ends | Khmer ។ ៕ ? ! …; a period after Thai or Lao letters never ends a sentence (abbreviations: พ.ศ., อย.). |
| Font weights | A system font's weight range is declared only when its `wght` axis reaches 400 (Android 15's Noto Sans Khmer has 26-190). |

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Word starts | The lookup starts at the start of the word under the aim as Android's ICU word segmentation finds it, and at the starts of the two ICU words before it; a word from an earlier start that reaches the aimed one comes first, the earliest start first, then the aimed word's own results (the Vietnamese rule). Two earlier words reach every part of 96% of Thai headwords; three lookups per tap (owner, 2026-10-19, question 1, after asking whether it is the most optimal and intuitive of the four: yes) |
| Anki sentence | Thai and Lao: a space between two letters of the script ends the sentence, except next to the repetition mark, ฯลฯ, digits and Latin words; ? ! … and line ends as well. Khmer: ។ ៕ ? ! … (owner, 2026-10-19, question 2) |
| Thai frequency | Built on the phone from PyThaiNLP's `tnc_freq.txt` (Thai National Corpus counts, CC0) at a pinned commit, checked by SHA-256, as the wordfreq lists are; Lao and Khmer have none (owner, 2026-10-19, question 3) |
| Paddle models upload | The Thai model and every other converted model that waits (Devanagari included) go up in one release later, when the owner decides; until then the catalog lists none of them and Thai offers Tesseract (owner, 2026-10-19, question 4) |
| Lao and Khmer | Thai and Khmer now, branches in the order Thai, Khmer; Khmer offers Tesseract on the phone. Lao is deferred, for a while or for good: one English dictionary of 2,300 words, Tesseract errors above those of the Paddle Latin model rejected for Vietnamese, no recordings or voice. The app is to say somewhere that on-device recognition of these languages may be poor; the wording and the place are settled later (question 9) (owner, 2026-10-19, question 5, first all three now, changed the same day after a summary of the three; replaces "later Lao, Khmer" of the languages plan's question 30) |
| Fonts | The system Noto Sans and Serif by default; downloadable: Thai Sarabun and Noto Sans Thai Looped (looped), Kanit and Prompt (loopless); Khmer Kantumruy Pro and Hanuman; Lao Noto Sans Lao Looped (OFL, from their authors' repositories as the other catalog fonts) (owner, 2026-10-19, question 6) |
| Shorter matches | The longest match from a start always shows; shorter ones from the same start only when they end at an ICU word boundary (การตลาด and การ stay, กา and ก go). Tested so that short words are not lost (owner, 2026-10-19, question 7) |
| Bubble letters | ท (Thai), ລ (Lao), ខ (Khmer): a letter of each language's own name, three distinct shapes (agent's choice at the owner's request, "whatever is better for recognizing the language", 2026-10-19, question 8) |

## Questions

1. Answered: (a), after the owner asked whether it is the most optimal and intuitive one (yes: any part of a word
   finds the whole word, with three lookups). Word starts. No spaces between words; the median word is 7 letters, so a
   tap lands mid-word. (a, recommended) ICU words and two earlier ones, as Vietnamese: ห้องสมุด tapped on สมุด shows
   "library" first, then "notebook"; 96% of Thai headwords found from any part; 3 lookups per tap. (b) The same with
   three earlier words: 99%, 4 lookups. (c) As Japanese, from the aimed letter (the languages plan's sketch). (d)
   Every start within 16 characters before the aim, the longest word covering it first: no ICU, about 16 lookups, more
   junk.
2. Answered: (a). The Anki sentence in Thai and Lao, which have no full stop: (a, recommended) a space between two
   letters ends it, except next to ๆ, ฯลฯ, digits and Latin, plus ! ? and line ends; a long sentence is sometimes cut
   to a clause. (b) The whole paragraph up to ! ? or its end.
3. Answered: (a). Thai frequency: wordfreq has none of the three. (a, recommended) PyThaiNLP's TNC list (106k words,
   CC0, 1.6 MB; 954 of its top 1,000 words are headwords), built on the phone as wordfreq's. (b) No frequency.
4. Answered: upload every model that is needed, in one release later, when the owner decides. The Paddle Thai model
   (7.9 MB; 1.3% errors against Tesseract's 21%): (a, recommended) upload it with the waiting Devanagari one when the
   Thai branch is ready; (b) Thai only; (c) no upload.
5. Answered: (a), with a note to add later; changed the same day to (b) with no date for Lao, which may be dropped
   for good. Lao and Khmer now or later: Khmer has km-en 9.2k and km-ru 5.9k words,

6. Answered: (a). Fonts: none in the catalog; the system has Noto Sans and Serif for each (Thai looped, Lao Sans
   loopless). (a, recommended) System plus popular downloadable ones (the row "Fonts"); (b) system only.
7. Answered: (a), to be tested so that short words are not lost. Shorter matches under the found word: (a,
   recommended) only those ending at an ICU word boundary; (b) every one from the same start, as Japanese (การตลาด
   brings การ, กา and the letter ก).

## Questions for the owner (overnight)

Asked when the owner writes again, in Russian, the recommended option first.

10. Decided provisionally, implemented: a space next to any ฯ (paiyannoi, the abbreviation mark: กรุงเทพฯ เป็น…)
    keeps the Anki sentence going, not only next to ฯลฯ as the row "Anki sentence" says, since ฯ shortens a word
    the sentence goes on after. Changing it back is one condition in `ThaiSupport.endsSentence` and a test.
    (a, recommended) keep: any ฯ; (b) only ฯลฯ, as decided.
11. As decided, implemented; asked because the emulator showed its cost: a space next to ๆ never ends a sentence,
    so a sentence ending in ๆ (…ไปโรงเรียนจริงๆ การตลาด…) runs on into the next one. In wty th-en's 4,466 example
    sentences (one sentence each) ๆ is followed by a space and more of the sentence 65 times (เด็กๆ ชอบ…) and ends
    the sentence 34 times. (a, recommended) keep; (b) end the sentence after ๆ followed by a space, which would cut
    the 65 (เด็กๆ | ชอบ…).
12. Decided provisionally, implemented (a bug fix found on the emulator): Android's word segmentation takes some
    Khmer phrases as one word (ក្រុងភ្នំពេញ "the city of Phnom Penh", នៅផ្ទះ "at home", រាល់ថ្ងៃ "every day"), which
    dictionaries have in parts, so aiming at ពេញ showed only ក្រុង (city). Now, when nothing found from the word's
    start reaches the aimed letter, the lookup also starts inside the word, letter cluster by cluster up to the aimed
    one, and the first start that finds a word covering it wins: ភ្នំពេញ (Phnom Penh) first, then ក្រុង. Thai gets the
    same. (a, recommended) keep; (b) always add the words found inside, also when the joined word is in a
    dictionary (more entries, more lookups per aim); (c) no starts inside a word (as before: ក្រុង only).
13. Open, a resource question: the emulator's /data has about 390 MB free with the languages of groups 1-4c
    installed; a full debug build no longer installs, so this run installed an x86_64-only copy (82 MB). Deleting
    dictionaries in the emulator's app was refused by the session's permission check as shared test state.
    (a, recommended) allow later sessions to delete the dictionaries of languages already checked (they download
    again in seconds); (b) keep installing stripped builds and leave the data; (c) enlarge the AVD's data partition
    (a wipe, which loses the emulator's state, including AnkiDroid's test collection).
14. Decided provisionally, implemented: Khmer keeps the default audio order of languages without spaces (Lingua
    Libre, Wiktionary, then the phone's voice), though neither site has recordings of common Khmer words, so two
    requests that find nothing come before the voice. (a, recommended) keep, as a recording may appear;
    (b) the voice first for Khmer.
