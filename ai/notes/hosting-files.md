# Files to host

Every file the project plans to serve from its own hosting, collected from the plans, notes and scripts on
2026-10-29. Hosting is postponed with no date (languages plan, request 30 and open item "Hosting"; remind the owner
of it whenever they ask what is left open). Until it exists the catalog lists none of the dictionaries below, test
builds load them from a local address (`adb push`), and nothing goes on a public host without asking the owner. The
one place in use is the release `ocr-models-1` of the screenlate repository, which holds converted Paddle models only.
A session that builds, rebuilds or uploads one of these files updates its row here.

Plan files named below:

- languages plan: `plans/2026-10-10-languages-implementation.md`;
- OCR plan: `plans/2026-10-17-ocr-engines.md`;
- group 4b to 5e: `plans/2026-10-19-group-<block>.md`;
- group 6: `plans/2026-10-20-group-6-research.md`.

"Approved" means the owner approved the upload of that file. Sizes are as the sources give them; "unknown" where they
give none. Copies of the built files and their sources are in the private dictionary archive
(`notes/dictionary-archive.md`).

## Not hosted

- The Hanja dictionary stays in the APK for good (168 KB), also once hosting exists (languages plan, row "Hanja
  dictionary in the APK").
- Frequency dictionaries from wordfreq are built on the phone from wordfreq's word list at a pinned commit, checked by
  SHA-256 (languages plan, row "Frequency dictionaries" of 2026-10-14, which replaced their hosting).
  `scripts/frequency/wordfreq_dictionary.py` still takes `--download-url` and `--index-url` for a hosted archive;
  nothing uses them.
- The other frequency lists are built on the phone as well: Thai from the TNC list (group 4c, row "Thai frequency"),
  Kazakh and Kyrgyz from Leipzig (group 4e, row "Frequency"), Mongolian from Leipzig (group 5d, row "Mongolian
  frequency", which replaced a PC-built list hosted with the MonWN dictionary), Latin and Esperanto (group 5a,
  questions 23 and 28), Galician, Afrikaans, Tatar and Bashkir (group 6, question 7). Welsh, Irish and Scottish
  Gaelic get none (group 5b, question 6: built and hosted lists were not chosen).
- Tesseract's traineddata come unchanged from the tesseract-ocr repositories at a pinned commit (languages plan, row
  "Paddle model hosting"; `scripts/ocr-models/tessdata_catalog.py`).
- The OCR runtimes (Tesseract, MNN) are in the APK (languages plan, row "On-device OCR engines"); the open item of
  `plans/2026-10-09-more-languages.md` that listed OCR runtime libraries for hosting is superseded.
- Paddle models not offered: `eslav` (lacks ѝ, worse on Ukrainian; group 5d, technical choice "Recognition";
  `paddle_models.py`) and the Kannada `ka_PP-OCRv3_mobile_rec` (misreads stacked consonants, 37.9% errors; group 5e,
  technical choice "Paddle models": not offered and not uploaded).
- FreeDict zh → ru is built (5.5 MB) but left out of the catalog: 74% of its senses are lists of unrelated words
  (languages plan, row "FreeDict into Russian", question 18).
- No Tatar dictionary of ours: wty lists Tatar since its release 2026.10.22 (group 6, question 14).
- No Breton dictionary: Breton is dropped (group 5b, question 5); our own converter from kaikki was not chosen.
- Files of the private dictionary archive without a license to pass on (JPDB v2.2, Aozora Bunko, jitai, the NINJAL
  lists BCCWJ, NWJC, CSJ, CEJC) are for our own use only. KRDICT's and STDICT's data is CC BY-SA, but their Yomitan
  packaging states no license (`notes/dictionary-archive.md`, "Sharing and licenses").
- Catalog dictionaries with releases of their own are downloaded from their publishers; the hosting is for files the
  app cannot download from their authors (languages plan, request 30).

## Open

- Where. Options in the plans: a release of the screenlate repository that holds model files only and is kept out of
  the update check (decided for the Paddle models, row "Paddle model hosting"; in use as `ocr-models-1`); a separate
  public repository for every file we build (question 31, option b; the open item says "likely a public repository of
  our own"); Hugging Face ("to be looked at"). Nothing is decided for the dictionaries and the audio packs.
- Approval. Nothing is uploaded before the owner approves the file list (row "Paddle model hosting"); a build for the
  owner to try loads nothing from a public host without asking first (open item "Hosting"). Approved so far: the seven
  archives on `ocr-models-1`.
- When. The waiting Paddle models go up in one release when the owner decides (group 4c, question 4); hosting for the
  rest has no date (request 30).
- Settled 2026-10-29: `ocr-models-1` is a regular release that is never marked latest (GitHub, and the languages plan's
  changelog of 2026-10-19); the waiting Paddle models go into it (group 4b, question 9); `docs/development.md` and the
  OCR plan said "prerelease" and are corrected.
- The private dictionary archive: the Backlog item says its copies are served from our hosting once it exists,
  `notes/dictionary-archive.md` says nothing from it goes into any upload.
- Non-commercial files (the Zaliznyak meta dictionary, Monier-Williams): for an app-store build they "move to
  self-hosting or are removed" (`notes/store-publishing.md`; decision "Risky dictionaries").
- NOTICE entries are added with each file's first upload, as the tables list them.
